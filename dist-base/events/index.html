Private hire

Your party, our place

The private function room seats parties for lunches, dinners and celebrations, while the terrace hosts larger summer gatherings with the course as a backdrop. We arrange menus, bar packages, entertainment and decorations.

  • Birthdays, anniversaries and wakes
  • Society and club presentation dinners

Community

A clubhouse that gives back

Casa Club regularly hosts local charities and community groups: Age Concern lunches and fairs, FMA fundraisers, a monthly Bereavement Café, kids’ talent shows and Camposol Golf Association competitions. If you run a group that needs a venue, talk to us.
