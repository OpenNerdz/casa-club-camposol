Camposol Club de Golf

Eighteen holes with the mountains for company

A par-72 parkland course in a sheltered valley of lakes, palms and olive groves, ten minutes from the coast and open all year round.

Book a tee timeCall the golf office

The course

Friendly on the front, testing on the back

Opened in 2002 and designed by Manuel Quesada Mendiola, the course climbs gently away from the clubhouse before turning back through water and desert-style scrub. The signature seventh is a par four played over the lake; the back nine tightens up with doglegs and well-placed bunkers. Wide fairways and a maximum handicap of 54 make it welcoming for society days and improvers, while the white tees (slope 136) give low handicappers plenty to think about.

Facilities

Everything for a full day of golf

Driving range

Recently refurbished range with covered bays, plus putting and chipping greens next to the clubhouse.

Crazy golf

A new 18-hole crazy-golf course beside Casa Club, perfect for families, parties and non-golfers.

Golf academy

PGA-qualified coaching for beginners to single-figure players: individual lessons, group clinics and junior sessions.

Societies & groups

Bring your society, we will do the rest

Camposol hosts visiting societies, corporate days and charity events throughout the year, with group green fees, prize tables, and lunch or dinner on the terrace afterwards. The Camposol Golf Association plays its weekly competition on Mondays and welcomes new members of all standards.

  • Group green-fee packages
  • Set menus from a bacon roll to a three-course dinner
  • Prize-giving in the function room
