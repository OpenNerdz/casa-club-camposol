Welcome

Eat, drink, play and celebrate

Casa Club is Camposol’s social hub: a relaxed, friendly clubhouse where neighbours, golfers and visitors meet from breakfast through to the last song of the night.

Restaurant & bar

Breakfasts, a weekday menú del día, Friday fish & chips and the famous Sunday carvery, all served on the big terrace or in the dining room.

Explore the restaurant

Golf

An 18-hole, par-72 parkland course with lakes, palms and mountain views, plus a refurbished driving range, academy and crazy golf for all ages.

Play the course

Live events

Quiz nights, bingo, live music every Friday, tribute acts, karaoke and summer BBQs. There is always something happening at Casa Club.

Restaurant & bar

Honest food, generous plates, sunshine on the side

From a proper breakfast before your round to a long Sunday lunch with the family, the kitchen serves British and Spanish favourites made fresh every day. Friendly service, fair prices and a terrace with one of the best views in Camposol.

Golf

Eighteen holes in the Murcian sun

Designed by Manuel Quesada, Camposol Club de Golf winds through a valley of lakes, olive groves and palms with the Sierra de las Moreras as a backdrop. A friendly test for every handicap, with a tougher back nine for those who want it.
