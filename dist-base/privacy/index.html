What we collect and why

When you use the contact form, call, email or message us on WhatsApp, we process the details you give us (name, phone, email, requested date and message) solely to answer your enquiry and manage reservations or events. The legal basis is your consent and, where applicable, the steps needed to provide the service you request (Art. 6.1.a and 6.1.b GDPR).

Retention

Enquiry data is kept for as long as needed to deal with your request and for up to one year afterwards, unless a longer period is required by law (for example, invoicing records).

Recipients

Your data is not sold or shared with third parties for marketing. If the contact form is routed through a form-delivery service, that provider processes the message on our behalf under a data-processing agreement. WhatsApp messages are handled by Meta under its own terms.

Your rights

You may request access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability or object to processing at any time by writing to eventos@casaclubcamposolgolf.com. You can also lodge a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (www.aepd.es).

Cookies and third-party services

This website does not use advertising or analytics cookies. The map is loaded from OpenStreetMap/CARTO tile servers and web fonts from Google Fonts; these providers receive your IP address when the page loads. Links to Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp and our tee-time booking partner lead to external sites with their own policies.
