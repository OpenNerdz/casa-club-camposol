## Venue identity (confirmed)

- **Name:** Casa Club Camposol (also styled "Casa Club Camposol Golf"; Google/TripAdvisor still list it under the old name "The Club House").
- **What it is:** the clubhouse bar / restaurant / function room of Camposol Golf (18-hole course, formerly Sensol Golf), with a terrace over the course, live music, quizzes, karaoke, menú del día, and a crazy-golf / mini-golf.
- **Address:** Calle Aljibe s/n, Urbanización Camposol Sector C, 30875 Mazarrón, Región de Murcia. Google Maps cid 3717581127948674934 (37.670098, -1.354977).
- **Phone / WhatsApp:** +34 602 42 01 67 (Casa Club bookings). Pro shop / golf: +34 624 41 38 01.
- **Email:** eventos@casaclubcamposolgolf.com (from the Facebook About tab). No standalone venue website was found (casaclubcamposol.com/.es/.net do not resolve).
- **Company:** Casa Club Camposol Golf SL, CIF B19345867, incorporated 2024 (Iberinform).
- **Social:** Facebook https://www.facebook.com/theclubhouse.camposol/ ("Casa Club Camposol | Mazarrón", category Restaurante); Instagram https://www.instagram.com/casaclubcamposol/ (8 posts); TikTok @casa.club.camposo.

## Branding observed

- **Logo:** round badge — mid-green ring (approx. #3f9b6f / #4f6b39 in the sign photo) with a white inner disc, two crossed golf clubs over a golf ball, "CASA CLUB" above and "CAMPOSOL GOLF" below in small caps. It appears on the welcome sign, every poster and the FB cover.
- **Signage type:** black Times-style serif capitals on a white board ("CASA CLUB / CAMPOSOL / GOLF"), with "Welcome to" in a script face.
- **Poster palette:** dark/black backgrounds with gold, and bright colour-blocked weekly planners (orange, purple, green, blue, cyan). camposolgolf.com uses near-black (#03090a, #242424) with white, fonts Poppins and Adamina.
- **Photographic palette:** terracotta/ochre clubhouse walls with yellow-ochre roof edges, white colonnade/pergola, green fairways, palms, blue sky, and the grey-brown Sierra de las Moreras.
